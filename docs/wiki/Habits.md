# Habits

In the **Habits** tab you track your own habits next to the running plan: stretching, sleep, water, a month without alcohol, whatever helps you.

## Creating

At the bottom you enter a name (up to 30 characters) and choose the type:

- **Check off**: the habit works out or it doesn't, for example “Stretch”.
- **Number**: you enter a value every day, for example “Sleep” in hours. You also give a unit (up to 12 characters).

Without any habits, the app suggests four: stretch, sleep (h), water (liters) and no alcohol.

## Logging

The table shows the last 35 days; today is on the far right. “← Earlier days” goes further back.

- **Check off**: tapping cycles through ✕ (done), ○ (not done) and empty.
- **Number**: tapping opens an input field.
- Tap the **name** to edit the habit.

## Streaks and average

Below each name there is a short summary:

- For check-off habits: “N days in a row” from a streak of two days, otherwise how often you did it in the last seven days.
- For numbers: the average of the last seven days with its unit.

The data lives in the data set under `habits` and `habitLog`, see [[Data model|Data-Model]].
