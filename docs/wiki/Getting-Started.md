# Getting started

## The first five minutes

1. **Open Settings** (icon at the top right). Choose your **language** (English or German) and enter your **race day** and **goal time**. These give your plan's calendar and the goal pace for the tempo sessions. See [[Settings|Settings]].
2. Optionally enter a **body part you want to watch**, for example your knee. After every run the app then asks how it feels. Leave it empty to turn this off.
3. Choose your **extra sessions** (Monday, Tuesday, Friday), for example strength or bouldering.
4. In the **Training** tab, log your first run or record it with GPS.
5. Save a **backup** regularly, see [[Backup and data|Backup-and-Data]].

## Defaults of a new installation

- Language **English**.
- Only **strength on Monday**; Tuesday and Friday are free.
- No watched body part.
- Race day 27 weeks after next Monday, on a Sunday.
- GPS privacy zone of 300 m around the start, route is saved.
- The diagnostic log is on. It contains no personal data, see [[Privacy|Privacy]].

Data from an earlier version (before the language setting existed) opens in English too. Switch to German once under Settings, Language; all entries stay as they are.

## How the app is organized

| Area | Content |
|---|---|
| **Training** | progress, this week, strength & stability, the whole plan |
| **Habits** | your own habits, streaks, weekly average |
| **Stats** | key figures, charts, map of your routes, weekly overview per sport, learnings |
| **Settings** | language, plan, GPS, diagnostics, records, goals (icon at the top right) |

At the top right there is also the **Save backup** / **All saved** button. The app saves your entries on the device immediately. The button only shows whether something was added since the last backup.
